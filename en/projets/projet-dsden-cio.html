Case study

CIO network optimisation

Simplifying and stabilising a local network infrastructure within DSDEN.

  • Patch cabinet
  • VLAN
  • Routing
  • Switches
  • Continuity

Context and goals

The existing infrastructure relied on fragmented and ageing interconnections, making operations harder to read, maintain and evolve.

Technical solutions

Rationalise switches and interconnections.

Harmonise VLAN and routing logic while anonymising sensitive details.

Clean up the physical patching to make future maintenance easier.

Main stages

  1. Audit the physical and logical network state.
  2. Identify obsolete or redundant equipment.
  3. Plan the migration to preserve service continuity.
  4. Apply the new interconnections and addressing logic.
  5. Validate stability and document the final architecture.

Benefits and results

  • Clearer physical and logical architecture.
  • Reduced maintenance complexity.
  • Better network stability and performance.
  • A more sustainable base for future supervision and QoS work.
