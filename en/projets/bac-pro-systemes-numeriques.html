OffiTouch — Pharmagest

Installing interactive connected window displays for pharmacies: HD touchscreen, Lenovo mini-PC, LGPI synchronisation, ticket printing and maintenance.

Technical features

  • Screen, Wall or Stand deployment depending on the pharmacy.
  • MySQL database, Microsoft tools and remote administration.
  • Controlled installation, automatic startup and maintenance.

Impact: a clearer interactive customer experience with no heavy building work.
