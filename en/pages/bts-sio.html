BTS SIO, SISR track

The BTS SIO is a French higher technical diploma for IT services in organisations. My track is SISR: infrastructure, systems and networks.

What is the BTS SIO?

The programme trains IT technicians who can help deploy, operate and support business IT services.

It combines IT fundamentals, project work, cybersecurity, user support, documentation and professional communication.

Why it matters here

This page connects my projects with the skills expected in the diploma, so the portfolio reads as a coherent technical file.

My direction

Why SISR?

My projects show a hands-on progression: automated workstation deployment, service hosting, network optimisation, maintenance and user assistance.

I value technical environments where reliability, documentation and maintainability matter as much as implementation.

SISR skills highlighted

  • Contribute to local network administration.
  • Automate deployment and configuration tasks.
  • Write procedures that other technicians can reuse.
  • Take security and data privacy into account.
